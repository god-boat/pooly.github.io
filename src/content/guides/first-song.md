## What you'll do

You'll build a short loop with drums, bass, and a melody using Pooly's built-in sounds. You won't need to import anything. You'll play the pads and draw a few notes in the editor.

## Step 1: Create a new project

From the **Pools** screen, tap **New Project** in the footer. Pooly opens the Launcher, the grid where you'll build and play your loop.

Your new project already has four tracks (drums, bass, chords, and melody), each with a randomly picked sound. You can swap any of them from the **Browser** later. To name your song, rename it from its card on the Pools screen.

## Step 2: Program a beat

Tap an **empty slot** in the first scene of the drum track. Pooly creates a clip and opens **Detail View** for editing.

Draw notes in the **piano roll**, or finger drum on the **performance pads** while the song plays and keep the take with the capture bar. Try a simple pattern:

- Kick on beats 1 and 3
- Snare on beats 2 and 4
- Hi-hat on every eighth note

Tap **Play** in the footer to hear your loop. On a phone, tap **Back** to return to the launcher when you're happy.

## Step 3: Add a bass line

Tap an empty slot in the same scene on the bass track. Draw a bass line that follows your kick. Short notes on the root are an easy start.

## Step 4: Add a melody

Tap an empty slot in the same scene on the melody track. Draw a melody over your beat. Four bars is plenty for a first song.

## Step 5: Launch everything together

Make sure each track has a clip in the same scene. Turn on **Show Scene Launcher** in Settings under **App** to show the scene buttons beside the grid, then tap the button for that scene. All its clips start together. If nothing plays, press **Play** first, or turn on **Auto Play on Trigger** so tapping a clip starts the transport.

## Tips

- Tap an empty slot to create and edit; **double tap** an existing clip to reopen it
- Keep patterns to 1, 2, or 4 bars while learning
- Use the **Mixer** button to balance levels once all tracks are playing
