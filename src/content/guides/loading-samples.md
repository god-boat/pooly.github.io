## What you'll do

You'll add an audio track and load a file from your device, then play it back in your project.

## Step 1: Add an audio track

Open a project and go to the Launcher. Tap the **Audio** add-track button at the end of the grid. Pooly creates a new audio track and opens Detail View so you can load a file.

## Step 2: Load your file

In Detail View you'll see the **wave editor**. Tap **Load Sample** and pick a file from your device—Pooly supports **WAV**, **OGG**, and **MP3**. You can also **Record** something new or **Resample** what Pooly is playing.

Your audio appears as a waveform and Pooly works out its tempo. Press **Play** to hear it. If the clip is longer than you need, trim it by adjusting the clip's start and end in the editor.

Prefer browsing? The **Samples** tab in the Browser lists sounds from your linked folders—select one and press **Load**.

## Step 3: Play it in the launcher

Head back to the Clip Launcher. Your audio clip sits in the slot you chose. Tap it to launch playback, or include it in a scene with your other tracks.

To load a different file into the same clip, **double tap** it to reopen it and use the load control in the wave editor.

## Step 4: Fit it to your song

Audio clips can follow your project's tempo using the warp mode in the clip controls. For loops, try **Beats**, **Repitch**, **Tones**, **Texture**, or **Complex** until the loop sits cleanly on the grid.

## Tips

- Clean, short samples work best when you're starting out
- One-shot hits are ideal for layering on a drum track
- Link your sample folder in the Browser so your sounds are always a tap away
