## What you'll do

You'll render your project to a file you can share, usually a WAV.

## Step 1: Decide what to export

Export renders your **Arranger** timeline, so lay out the song there first. The export dialog lets you choose which **bars** to render. By default it covers the whole timeline.

You can also export **MIDI** if you want to move your notes into another app. WAV is the usual choice for listening and sharing.

## Step 2: Open export

Tap the **song avatar** in the header to open **Settings**. On the **Project** tab, tap **Export**. An export dialog appears with file type and bar-range options.

Playback stops while you set up the export.

## Step 3: Render

Choose **WAV** (or **MIDI**), confirm the bar range, and tap **Export**. Pooly asks where to save the file, then renders the song and saves it. Editing is paused until it finishes.

Rendering time depends on song length and how many tracks and effects you used.

## Step 4: Share

Open the file from your device's files app to play or share it. Listen on headphones before you send it to check for clipping and level problems.

## Tips

- Export **WAV** when quality matters most
- Check the bar range if your file comes out shorter or longer than expected
- Balance levels in the **Mixer** before exporting
