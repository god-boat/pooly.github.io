## What you'll do

You'll use scenes to trigger groups of clips at once—like switching between a verse and a chorus during a live jam.

## Step 1: Fill a scene with clips

In the Clip Launcher, each **scene** lines up one slot from every track. Put one clip per track in the same scene. Empty slots simply stay silent when you launch it.

## Step 2: Show the scene launcher

Open Settings from the song avatar and turn on **Show Scene Launcher** under **App**. Numbered scene buttons appear beside the grid.

## Step 3: Launch a scene

Press **Play** if transport isn't already running (unless you turned on **Auto Play on Trigger** in Settings). Tap the button for the scene you want. Every clip in that scene starts together, right on the beat.

Build more scenes for different sections—intro in scene 1, groove in scene 2, breakdown in scene 3—and switch between them by launching different scenes.

## Step 4: Mix while you play

You don't have to launch whole scenes every time. Tap individual clips to trigger just one track. Open the **Mixer** to mute, solo, or ride faders live—or pick the **Launcher + Mixer** layout to keep both on screen. Muting the drums for one scene while keeping bass and keys is an easy way to create a breakdown.

## Step 5: Build a timeline later

When you're ready for a fixed song structure, switch to the **Arrangement** view and place clips on the timeline bar by bar. Scenes are great for jamming and trying ideas; the arranger is where you lay out the final version.

## Tips

- Keep clip lengths similar (4 or 8 bars) if you want scenes to feel cohesive, or vary the lengths for a more dynamic performance
- Leave a slot empty in a scene when you want silence on that track
- Color tracks in the mixer or launcher so you can spot them quickly
