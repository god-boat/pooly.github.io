## What you'll do

You'll use scenes to trigger groups of clips at once, for example to switch from a verse to a chorus while you jam.

## Step 1: Fill a scene with clips

In the Clip Launcher, each **scene** lines up one slot from every track. Put one clip per track in the same scene. Empty slots stay silent when you launch the scene.

## Step 2: Show the scene launcher

Open Settings from the song avatar and turn on **Show Scene Launcher** under **App**. Numbered scene buttons appear beside the grid.

## Step 3: Launch a scene

Press **Play** if transport isn't already running (unless you turned on **Auto Play on Trigger** in Settings). Tap the button for the scene you want. Every clip in that scene starts together, on the beat.

Build a scene for each section (intro in scene 1, groove in scene 2, breakdown in scene 3) and launch them in whatever order you like.

## Step 4: Mix while you play

You can also tap a single clip to change just that track. Open the **Mixer** to mute, solo, or ride faders as you play, or pick the **Launcher + Mixer** layout to keep both on screen. Muting the drums while the bass and keys keep going makes an easy breakdown.

## Step 5: Build a timeline later

Once you know the order of your sections, switch to the **Arrangement** view and place clips on the timeline.

## Tips

- Clips of the same length (4 or 8 bars) loop in step. Mixed lengths drift against each other, which can sound good too
- Leave a slot empty in a scene when you want silence on that track
- Color tracks in the mixer or launcher so you can spot them quickly
