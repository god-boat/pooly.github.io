## What you'll do

You'll load a factory preset, change a few of its settings, and add effects.

## Step 1: Pick a starting sound

Open Detail View on a MIDI track by tapping an empty launcher slot, **double tapping** a clip, or tapping a track header. Then tap the **Browser** button in the header.

Pick the **Instruments** or **Drums** tab and browse by category (keys, bass, pads, leads, and so on), or search by name. Tap a preset, then tap it again or press **Load** to put it on the track. Press **Preview** to hear it first.

## Step 2: Shape the tone

Detail View shows pages of controls for the current instrument, such as voices, modulators, filters, and envelopes. Which pages you get depends on the sound. A few to start with:

- **Attack** controls how quickly the sound fades in
- **Release** controls how it fades out after you let go
- **Filter** pages brighten or darken the tone

Play the performance pads or piano roll to hear your changes as you go.

## Step 3: Add effects

With the Browser open, switch to the **FX** tab and add an effect, like a reverb, delay, or filter. Effects process the instrument's output.

Each effect shows up in Detail View's device bar. Tap one to adjust it. To see the whole chain or change the order, open the **Device Graph** from the header and drag effects around.

## Step 4: Save your work

Your changes are saved with the project. To use the sound in other projects, save it as a preset from the instrument's core panel. Saved presets show up under the **User** source in the Browser.

## Tips

- Pick a preset that's close to what you want, then adjust it
- Heart presets you use a lot to find them later in **Favorites**
