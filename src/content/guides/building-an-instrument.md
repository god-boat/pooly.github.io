## What you'll do

You'll start from a factory preset, tweak it to your taste, and add effects.

## Step 1: Pick a starting sound

Open Detail View on any MIDI track—tap an empty launcher slot, **double tap** a clip, or tap a track header. Then tap the **Browser** button in the header.

Pick the **Instruments** or **Drums** tab and browse by category—keys, bass, pads, leads, and more—or use search to find something close to what you want. Tap a preset, then tap it again or press **Load** to put it on the track. Press **Preview** first if you want a listen.

## Step 2: Shape the tone

Detail View shows pages of controls for the current instrument—voices, modulators, filters, envelopes, and more depending on the sound. Small moves go a long way:

- **Attack** controls how quickly the sound fades in
- **Release** controls how it fades out after you let go
- **Filter** pages brighten or darken the tone

Play the performance pads or piano roll to hear your changes as you go.

## Step 3: Add effects

With the Browser open, switch to the **FX** tab. Add a reverb for space, a delay for echoes, or a filter to sculpt the tone further. Effects process the sound after the instrument—think of them as pedals on a guitar.

Each effect shows up in Detail View's device bar—tap one to adjust it. Open the **Device Graph** from the header to see the whole chain and drag effects into a new order.

## Step 4: Save your work

Your tweaks live in the project—Pooly remembers them automatically. To reuse a sound elsewhere, save it as a preset from the instrument's core panel. Your saved presets appear under the **User** source in the Browser.

## Tips

- Start from a preset and nudge a few knobs rather than building from zero
- Heart the presets you keep coming back to so they're waiting in **Favorites**
