## 3.1 When to use the Clip Launcher

Use the Clip Launcher when you want to try ideas quickly, trigger clips live, or build song sections scene by scene. Projects open here. Each track has a lane of clip slots, and each scene lines up one slot from every track.

## 3.2 Layout

The launcher is a scrollable grid:

- **Tracks** each get a lane of slots (MIDI or audio)
- **Scenes** line up one slot from every track
- **Cells** each hold a clip or sit empty

Track headers sit at the end of each lane with name, color, and quick controls. Scene launch buttons sit beside the grid when the scene launcher is turned on. By default the grid follows your device's orientation. You can change it in Settings under **App → Time Axis Orientation**.

## 3.3 Adding tracks

Use the **MIDI** and **Audio** buttons at the end of the grid. MIDI tracks hold note clips played by synths, samplers, or drum kits. Audio tracks hold recorded or imported audio.

## 3.4 Working with clips

### 3.4.1 Create a clip

Tap an **empty slot** on a MIDI track. Pooly creates a clip and opens it in Detail View so you can add notes. On audio tracks, an empty slot opens the wave editor, ready for you to load a file, record, or resample.

### 3.4.2 Launch a clip

Tap a clip that already has content to **launch** it on that track. If transport isn't running, press **Play** first, or turn on **Auto Play on Trigger** in Settings under **App** so launching a clip starts playback automatically.

### 3.4.3 Edit a clip

**Double tap** a clip to open it in Detail View, or **long-press** it and choose **Edit**.

Tapping a track header also opens Detail View for that track, showing the clip that's playing. If nothing is playing, it shows the first clip on the track, or the first empty slot if the track has no clips yet.

### 3.4.4 Other clip actions

Long-press a clip for **Clone Clip**, **Duplicate**, **Rename Clip**, **Linked Editing**, **Stop**, and **Delete**.

### 3.4.5 Move and duplicate clips

**Long-press** a clip and drag to move it between slots and tracks. Use the long-press menu to duplicate or clone without dragging.

## 3.5 Working with scenes

### 3.5.1 Show scene buttons

Turn on **Show Scene Launcher** in Settings under **App** to show the numbered scene buttons beside the grid.

### 3.5.2 Launch a scene

Tap a scene button to launch every clip in that scene at once. Empty slots stay silent. Playback won't start on its own unless **Auto Play on Trigger** is on.

## 3.6 Track headers

Tap a track header to select the track and open it in Detail View. **Long-press** a header for actions such as fold, stop, change color, move, duplicate, or delete. **Long-press** then drag a header to reorder tracks.

## 3.7 Tips

- Pinch the grid to zoom
- Fold tracks you aren't working on to make room for the ones you are
- Use the **Launcher / Arrangement** button in the header when you're ready to lay clips on a timeline
