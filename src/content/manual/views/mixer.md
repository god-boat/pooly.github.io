## 5.1 When to use the Mixer

Use the Mixer when clips are playing and you want to balance volume, adjust panning, or mute and solo tracks. It mirrors your project's track list—every track has a matching channel strip here.

## 5.2 Opening the Mixer

Tap **Mixer** in the footer. On a phone the Mixer fills the screen; on larger screens it opens beside your work or in a tab. Tap **Mixer** again to put it away. Pick the **Launcher + Mixer** layout to keep it open next to the clip grid.

## 5.3 Channel strips

Each track appears as a strip showing:

- The **track header** with name and color
- A **volume fader**
- **Pan** control
- **Mute** and **Solo** buttons
- A **level meter** showing signal while audio plays

## 5.4 Volume and pan

Drag a fader to raise or lower a track's level. Adjust pan to shift a sound left or right in the stereo field.

## 5.5 Mute and solo

**Mute** silences a track without deleting its clips. **Solo** focuses on one or more tracks by muting the others temporarily—useful when checking a single part during a dense mix.

## 5.6 The Main track

The **Main** strip sits pinned beside the other tracks. Every track plays through it, so its fader sets the overall level of your song. Tap its header to select it, then add effects in Detail View to shape the whole mix.

## 5.7 Track actions

Tap a track header to select it. **Long-press** a header to open the context menu: stop, change color, move, duplicate, or delete.

## 5.8 Adding tracks

The **MIDI** and **Audio** add buttons at the end of the strips work the same as in the Clip Launcher—create tracks without leaving the mixer.

## 5.9 Tips

- Mix at modest levels—leave headroom so the main output doesn't distort
- Use solo to check one element, then un-solo before exporting
